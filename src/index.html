Start in three steps

  1. Create your tree

    Sign in, give the tree a name, and add the oldest ancestor you know.

  2. Add the family

    Add children under each parent, with the years, the place and the story.

  3. Invite relatives

    Send a link. They sign in and join as an editor or a viewer, as you choose.

Your family's information stays yours

How your tree is protected
  • Trees can be read only by the people who belong to them. Database rules enforce this on the server.
  • Only owners can invite people, rename the tree or delete it. Viewers cannot change anything.
  • An invite link works only for someone who is signed in. You choose the role the link gives.
  • There are no ads and no trackers. This app does not load scripts from advertising or analytics companies.
